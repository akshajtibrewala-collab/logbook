Glass buttons (candidates)

Buttons use a “lite glass” recipe with no backdrop-filter: a translucent tint, a curved specular rim (brighter top-left and bottom-right), a thin inner highlight, an inner glow and a soft outer shadow. Real blur stays on the tab bar, top bar and the one open sheet or popover. Sky = pilot primary, violet = passenger primary, clear = secondary, restrained red only on a destructive confirm. Solid, Reduce transparency, prefers-contrast and browsers without backdrop-filter get solid tinted fills with the same contrast.

What the web approximates. Apple’s glass refracts whatever is behind it, takes its highlight from the device’s light and motion, re-tints from the pixels under it every frame, and merges neighbouring controls into one liquid shape. Here the tint and rim are static CSS gradients and nothing behind a button bends, so it reads as glossy tinted glass rather than a lens. It is deliberately cheaper than the real thing and looks best on the dark, calm surfaces this app uses.
